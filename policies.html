Before Your Visit

Salon Policies

Health, Safety & Sanitation

Our non-negotiables

  • Single-use files, buffers, and pedicure liners for every client — never reused
  • Metal tools sterilized in an autoclave between every use
  • Pedicure tubs and stations disinfected before every appointment
  • All technicians licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR)

Appointments & Arrivals

We ask that you arrive 5–10 minutes before your appointment. If you're more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule so we don't affect the next client.

Cancellations

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule — that way we can offer the spot to someone else.

Illness Policy

Feeling under the weather? Please reschedule — we'll happily move your appointment with no penalty. It protects you, our staff, and the next client in the chair.

Patch Test & Allergies

Gel, dip powder, acrylic, and lash adhesive contain chemicals that can occasionally cause a reaction. If you have known sensitivities or it's your first time with a product, tell us before we begin — we're happy to do a patch test.

Right to Refuse Service

For the safety of our clients and staff, we may decline or discontinue a service if we notice signs of infection, open wounds, or a nail/skin condition that needs a doctor's attention first.

Minors & Guardian Consent

Clients under 18 need a parent or guardian present for their first visit, and children accompanying an adult must be supervised at all times.

Payment Methods

We accept cash and card. Cash tips are always welcome and deeply appreciated by our team.

Satisfaction

If something isn't quite what you expected, let us know within 48 hours — we'll make it right at no extra cost.

Photos & Portfolio

We may photograph finished nails or lashes for our portfolio and social media. Just let your technician know if you'd rather not be included — no problem at all.

A neighborhood salon in San Antonio, TX. Manicure, pedicure, nails, and lashes — backed by more than 30 years of experience.
